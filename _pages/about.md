## Yichen Li (李亦晨)
Yichen Li (李亦晨) is a Ph.D. Candidate (4<sup>th</sup> year student, 2023.09-2028.01) in School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by [Prof. Ruixuan Li](https://idc.hust.edu.cn/rxli/index.htm). Meanwhile, I am also a visiting scholar at 1) University of Cambridge, advised by [Prof. Ozgur B. Akan](https://oba.eco/) and 2) University of Tokyo, advised by [Prof. Renhe Jiang](https://www.renhejiang.com/). I also work closely with [Prof. Rui Zhang](https://www.ruizhang.info/) and [Prof. Haozhao Wang](https://wanghaozhao.mysxl.cn/) from HUST. 

My research focuses on distributed learning among mobile devices. I have published over 50 papers in top-tier conferences and journals including IEEE COMST, IEEE TPAMI, ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, and WWW with over 800 Google Scholar citations. I have led the Fundamental Research Project for Young Professional from National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) (<ins>国家自然科学基金青年学生基础研究项目, 博士研究生</ins>) and the Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities, HUST (<ins>研究生创新基金项目, 华中科技大学</ins>). I have served multiple times as a Program Committee member and reviewer for top AI conferences and journals. I was selected for the Youth Talents Support Project from China Association for Science and Technology (<ins>中国科协青年科技人才培育工程博士生专项计划</ins>).

## 📖 Research
I am passionate about **distributed learning** among mobile devices. My current research interests focus on five key areas:
- **<ins>⭐ Federated Continual / Incremental / Life-Long Learning</ins>** 
- Federated Learning (**_e.g.,_** Knowledge Distillation, Model Aggregation...)
- Recommendation System (**_e.g.,_** Sequential RecSys, Cold-Start Problem)
- Multi-Agent System (**_e.g.,_** Self-Evovling, FedSkill)
- ⭐ NEW! AI4S! (**_e.g.,_** Medicine, Gene)
